# Website strategy

## Recommended structure and navigation

Use a focused one-page academic site with: About, Research, Projects, Publications, Experience, Teaching, Personal, and Contact. News, innovation, funding, service, technical skills, and academic profile links appear as supporting sections without overcrowding the navigation.

## Homepage priorities

- Postdoctoral Researcher at ETRI and IEEE Senior Member
- 10+ years of systems and networking research
- Clear research identity: low-latency transport, intelligent networking, programmable networks, and Kubernetes edge-cloud orchestration
- Evidence of academic readiness: four courses taught, three graduate researchers mentored, international collaboration, patents, technology transfer, and proposal-development experience
- Selected publications and direct links to CV, Google Scholar, ORCID, LinkedIn, and email

## Secondary content

Detailed employment and education timelines, the broader publication list, project summaries, teaching interests, technical skills, journal reviewing, patents, news, and the personal gallery belong after the homepage narrative.

## Gaps in the former Google Site

- No strong research-impact summary or Assistant Professor positioning
- Publications hidden behind an accordion and not organized by year or type
- Teaching, mentoring, collaboration, funding, service, patents, and technology transfer were absent or underrepresented
- Project descriptions were lengthy and did not clearly connect each project to research outcomes
- The homepage mixed outdated membership wording and old contact details with recent updates
- No prominent CV download, ORCID, selected-publication area, or clear technical expertise summary
- The gallery lacked categories and a consistent visual layout

## Presentation recommendations

Lead with a coherent systems-research agenda rather than a list of technologies. Keep the strongest evidence visible above the fold. Distinguish selected publications from the complete record, update news selectively, and connect mentoring and teaching to concrete courses and outcomes. Add verified grant names and amounts only when documentation is available. Add citation metrics only when they can be refreshed regularly.
